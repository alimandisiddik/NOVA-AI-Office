# Vision

## Purpose

NOVA AI Office will become an executive multi-agent operating environment that helps leaders coordinate work, reason over trusted organizational context, and execute approved workflows with clear governance.

## Product principles

1. **Executive clarity** — convert complex work into concise, decision-ready outputs.
2. **Specialized collaboration** — assign clear responsibilities to purpose-built agents.
3. **Human control** — require explicit review for consequential actions.
4. **Trustworthy context** — make knowledge provenance, recency, and ownership visible.
5. **Secure by design** — apply least privilege, isolation, and auditable operations from the beginning.

## Long-term outcome

NOVA should function as a reliable digital executive office: capable of preparing, coordinating, and tracking work while preserving human accountability for decisions and approvals.

## Non-goals for Sprint 1.1

- Connecting communication or productivity tools beyond the local Telegram bot.
- Calling AI model providers.
- Automating business actions.
- Persisting sensitive business data.
